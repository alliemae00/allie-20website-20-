  • De Profundis
 

Some tigers want to hunt, and others want to play.

All Bruce the Tiger wanted to do was watch the birds.

Bruce wanted to fly.

He told his friends about the birds in his deep, rumbling tiger voice.

"I've never seen a tiger fly before," they said. "Tigers belong on the ground.".

Bruce never learned to listen, so he went and tried to learn how to fly anyway.

He felt silly on the first few tries, and the next few tries hurt.

He fell a lot, and the other tigers laughed.

Bruce brushed it off and tried again.

This time he flew, and soon he was climbing clouds and had tamed the sky.

No one had ever seen a tiger fly until Bruce learned how.

When the other tigers saw Bruce in the air, they wanted to fly too.

"Bruce, can you teach us to fly?"

Bruce smiled, winked, and took off. "Tigers belong on the ground, right?"

Now, when you want to see Bruce The Flying Tiger, all you have to do is watch the sky.

You’ll hear him coming and watch him soar.222
